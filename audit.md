# Independent engineering audit

Reviewed through **5 September 2026**. This records source findings and design judgments, not a running-system certification. The original local notes, PDFs, extracted papers and cloned trees were reviewed as provisional evidence. No CCTV software was installed, built or exercised during this review. Links below identify the source actually relevant to the decision; default-branch observations are distinguished from release code.

## Foundation decision: preserve a useful whole, own the product seams

**Recommend a controlled Frigate fork for the first local appliance, with a small, distinct product module inside it.** Frigate is a substantial foundation, not just a detector wrapper. Retain its media lifecycle, recording index, detector worker interface, tracking, zone evaluation, review timeline and web UI. Own incident identity, policy/version semantics, acknowledgement and verdicts, and eventually a durable outbound queue. Those modules can initially share the process and database. A microservice split is not required to establish ownership.

This is an engineering judgment about work saved, not a measured implementation-time claim. No estimate in person-weeks is justified before a build. Ordinary adaptation is expected. The RTMDet adapter gap is work to bound, not grounds to reject the foundation.

| Approach | Substantial code retained | Work we still own | Decision / switch criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Controlled Frigate fork | Camera workers, restream integration, recording/review lifecycle, local React/FastAPI UI, inherited Norfair and zones | Security patches, operator incident semantics, schedule/deduplication, reproducible packaging, later rule/model adapters | **Choose now.** Keep upstream history and a small patch series; disable unrelated AI features. No automatic commercial rewrite. |
| External service beside stock Frigate | Same appliance, via its HTTP/MQTT interfaces | Second UI/config and authorization boundary, durable reconciliation of notifications with event records | Sensible when integrating an existing customer installation. Less useful here: it leaves source/security changes unresolved and MQTT is not a complete per-frame track-history API. |
| First-party core around MediaMTX + FFmpeg, optionally DL Streamer | Mature protocol routing, recording primitives, decoders; Intel inference/tripwire elements if selected | Supervisor, camera onboarding, clock/frame correlation, event retention, storage reconciliation, review UI, auth and upgrades | Credible fallback, not “small glue.” Choose if a measured requirement needs independent CV scaling, deeper pipeline control, or unacceptable fork maintenance. Replace a boundary at a time. |
| Viseron or ZoneMinder foundation | Other working NVR/media implementations | Product policy and security work still required; different coupled internals | Viseron is a real permissive alternative, not rejected merely for overlap. No demonstrated advantage for this scope. ZoneMinder is valuable as an installed VMS adapter; its GPL stack and external AI path warrant a deliberate product/license choice. |

Keep **one restream owner and one authoritative incident writer**. The selected deployment has embedded go2rtc and Frigate/FFmpeg, not MediaMTX alongside them. The existing NVR keeps its archive; the edge additionally keeps the bounded evidence needed for its own incidents. It cannot assume arbitrary NVR replay/export APIs. If MediaMTX is later selected, its authenticated RTSP output can feed FFmpeg or GStreamer; it routes/remuxes compressed media, not decoded tensors. DL Streamer can replace a CV worker, but its GstAnalytics output still needs a conversion into our event/track contract.

### Hardening changes that affect the choice

1. [Credential/log advisory](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/security/advisories/GHSA-c4qf-xxq4-vf55) names 0.17.1 and no patched version. Independently, [v0.17.2 log routing](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/blob/v0.17.2/frigate/api/app.py) permits any authenticated user. The [wizard](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/blob/v0.17.2/web/src/components/settings/wizard/Step2ProbeOrSnapshot.tsx) passes secrets in GET queries. Admin-only logs alone are insufficient: probes need body-based input, redaction, cache/log controls and no generated passwords in logs. Secret persistence must reflect unattended restart: a root-readable mounted secret or an OS-backed store has a host-root trust boundary; an encrypted vault whose key lives beside it does not protect against host compromise.
2. [Static recording ACL advisory](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/security/advisories/GHSA-74x4-gw64-2mq5) lists all versions and no patch. [Stable nginx configuration](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/blob/v0.17.2/docker/main/rootfs/usr/local/nginx/conf/nginx.conf) serves recording/clip/export paths with generic auth and directory listing. Enforce camera authorization on every media path and disable listings/public caching. A single-operator lab does not prove multi-user isolation.
3. [Regex denial-of-service advisory](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/security/advisories/GHSA-q8jx-q884-jcq9) lists `<=0.17.2`, patched in `>=0.18.0-beta3`; its narrative contains older contradictory patch text. [Stable event filters](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/blob/v0.17.2/frigate/api/event.py) do feed user patterns into [Python regex on the SQLite worker](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/blob/v0.17.2/frigate/db/sqlitevecq.py). Remove unsupported regex filters or backport a verified fix. Feature disabling alone does not necessarily remove an API route.
4. [go2rtc RTSP parser advisory](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/security/advisories/GHSA-4xp7-86rr-9758) concerns embedded 1.9.14 and lists no fixed version. Do not “fix” stable by blindly upgrading every dependency. Check exact vendored parser/build and reachable listeners. Other June advisories do list 0.17.2 patches; do not incorrectly call all disclosed defects unpatched.

The proposed initial base is therefore **v0.17.2 plus an explicitly reviewed patch set and refreshed media builds**, not the stock image. Reassess the latest stable release when implementation starts. If this patch burden cannot stay bounded, the MediaMTX/core alternative becomes preferable. These are proposed changes; this research commit implements none of them.

## Field evidence and operational consequences

The [TfL full report](https://foi.tfl.gov.uk/FOI-3155-2324/Smart%20Station%20End%20of%20PoC%20Report_Redacted.pdf) includes image-only results pages; extracted text alone misses them. It supports a one-station operational trial with useful human action, intrusive litter alerts, disabled bicycle/scooter logic and staged weapons. Its voluntary “invalid” feedback is not a false-positive denominator. Power/switch, GPU and mobile-data failures reinforce the need for independent recorder continuity and offline operation. The [DHS full assessment](https://www.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/2023-01/22_0818_st_deepzero.pdf) is a half-day staged exercise with props and human verification, not a sensitivity study. [ALERTCalifornia](https://alertcalifornia.org/technology/) establishes a camera/AI/human workflow, not an independently measured sensitivity or nuisance-alert rate.

No vendor success count proves safety efficacy. No target hardware, authorized target footage, codec matrix, storage benchmark or prospective held-out-site evaluation is available in this repository. Those are the main unresolved engineering questions.

## Acceptance semantics to carry into code

- A **detection** is a box/class/score at a frame time. A **track** is a temporary within-camera identity; reboot/reconnect starts a new epoch. A **rule event** is a state transition. An **incident** groups related rule events for review. A **notification** is a delivery attempt. Acknowledged does not mean true positive, and no delivery acknowledgement means status unknown rather than success.
- For intrusion, use a documented ground-contact point (Frigate uses bottom-center), polygon, enabled policy and persistence. “Person in a restricted zone” is observable; trespass/intent is not. If a rule requires two seconds, its earliest qualifying time is already two seconds after entry.
- Define line crossing from signed side changes of the same valid trajectory, finite-line intersection, minimum displacement and hysteresis. Wrong-way adds an allowed direction. No line crossing may be inferred across a tracking gap. Dwell requires a clock, occlusion grace and reset rules. Frigate's current zones/loitering are reusable; arbitrary line/direction/schedule rules still need product work.
- Measure false **incidents** and delivered nuisance notifications per valid negative camera-hour separately, with rule, camera and whole-site totals. Do not improve the metric simply by dropping frames or suppressing alerts: report blind time, suppressed candidates, misses and queue age. A per-rule budget of one/day over eight cameras and four rules could allow 32 interruptions/day.
- Freeze calibration before blind testing, split by site/camera/date and keep source-adjacent clips together. Include long normal recordings, day/night, compression, rain, shadows, reflections, insects, small people, occlusion, staff and camera movement. Staged positives measure coverage under declared conditions, not natural-event recall.
- As an illustrative sampling limit, zero false incidents in 72 valid negative camera-hours gives a one-sided 95% Poisson upper bound of about `3/72 = 0.0417` per hour (one/day), assuming stationarity and independent events. It does not establish a cross-site guarantee. Use camera/day blocks to expose correlation and report intervals and sample counts alongside rates.
- Separate frame age, inference latency, rule persistence, incident commit, UI delivery and playable-evidence latency. Record source PTS and local monotonic arrival time; wall-clock corrections must not reverse dwell timers. The NVR timeline and separate main/substreams need offset checks. A 5 FPS sample rate is a starting workload, not proof that brief motion will be caught or decode load falls to 5 FPS.
