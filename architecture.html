05 / OWN THE INCIDENT

The event is our product boundary.

Retain Frigate's bottom-center zone evaluation and review/recording machinery. Add a distinct incident module with stable identity, policy version, explicit state, evidence readiness and a persistent operator verdict. It can live inside the fork.

The engineering contract
  • First: one person in one polygon for two seconds; one incident per continuous qualifying track/zone.
  • Five seconds of pre-roll and ten seconds after qualification, when available. Alert first; evidence may still be pending.
  • Track IDs reset with a camera epoch. Acknowledgement and true/false/uncertain verdict are separate states.

01 / Why start from Frigate?

It saves an entire media-to-review lifecycle. Preserve that useful whole and maintain a focused patch series. Our policy and incident modules are distinct even while sharing a process. Compare the foundations.

02 / What are we borrowing later?

RTMDet needs a real output/preprocessing adapter; ByteTrack must improve target tracking. MediaMTX or DL Streamer can replace a boundary if justified. They are not extra services in this map's selected stack.

03 / What is still unproven?

Target camera/hardware capacity, long-run false incidents and installation compatibility. Frigate stable needs source-backed hardening, including media access and secret handling. This map records a design, not a tested deployment.

Read the stream trace as text
  1. The existing camera continues feeding its NVR. The edge needs credentials and authorized access: discover with WS-Discovery UDP 3702 and ONVIF SOAP over HTTP(S), or enter an RTSP URL. NVR PoE isolation and proprietary cameras can require installer work.
  2. The edge initiates RTSP control; compressed RTP video returns, initially interleaved over TCP. go2rtc fans out each source. A main stream, substream and NVR can consume separate camera sessions.
  3. Compressed main video branches into Frigate/FFmpeg recording before inference. A bounded local evidence window is independent of the NVR archive. Substream decoding produces raw frames; sampling and motion/region gating feed the detector.
  4. Supported OpenVINO SSD inference produces boxes; inherited Norfair produces temporary tracks. Zone persistence and product policy qualify an incident. Record source PTS and monotonic arrival time; gaps and reconnects reset trajectory state.
  5. A single incident writer owns IDs, policy versions, metadata, clip state and verdicts. The recorder supplies segment indexes and playable evidence when available. Retention and full-disk failures must never leave falsely playable links.
  6. The desktop browser connects over authenticated HTTPS/WSS. Local media uses authenticated MP4/MSE initially; H.264 is the first codec. The browser posts configuration, acknowledgements and verdicts back to the edge. Local updates work without WAN while the page, LAN and edge work.
  7. Only if enabled, a durable outbox uses an edge-initiated authenticated TLS connection for allowlisted metadata/health and separately opted-in media. Hosted replies can deliver validated configuration and signed updates. WAN loss pauses this path; local rules and login continue. Browser-provider push is an external dependency too.
